Adulsa

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Justicia adhatoda (L)

Common Name: Adulsa / Vasaka / Malabar Nut

Family: Acanthaceae

Description

Adulsa is an evergreen shrub with green shrub with green leaves and white flowers. It is commonly found in India and is traditionally valued for its medicinal properties.

Medicinal Uses

  • Traditionally used for cough, cold and bronchitis-related problems.
  • Used traditionally for bronchitis and breathing-related complaints.
  • Traditionally used to help relieve respiratory congestion.
